# Crate v0.4.5

Patch release: memory-aware progressive buffering, a more deliberate crossfade,
PC-to-device artwork staging, a device sync/library UI refresh, and a flat
Bibliotecas redesign with player-color theming.

## Highlights

### Playback
- Progressive buffering now follows a memory-aware policy: a tolerant in-memory
  preload timeout falls back to file streaming, with a 512 MB reserve, a 128 MB
  small-track allowance, a 256 MB post-read safety floor, and a 2 GB per-track
  cap.
- Gapless dual-stream preloading keeps a second track resident in memory when
  the system still has more than 1 GiB free, while respecting the per-track cap
  and post-read floor.
- The crossfade volume dip is now a cue for *user-initiated* track changes only:
  automatic advances at the end of a track stay seamless, and no fade runs when
  crossfade is disabled or playback is paused.

### Devices and sync
- PC-to-device sync stages embedded artwork at the configured device limit
  before the final copy, avoiding a second device write for newly copied tracks.
  An explicit per-sync artwork selection overrides the persisted device default,
  and a resumed journal keeps its stored decision.
- Artwork optimization advances progress only on completed/failed steps, yields
  periodically during analysis, verifies resized covers against the limit, and
  preserves the audio extension on temp paths for tag writers.
- Artwork optimization owns its `optimizing_artwork` sync-progress phase, so the
  optimize panel no longer shows a duplicate header progress bar.
- The Devices route uses the shared top-chrome dashboard tabs, keeps the primary
  device navbar above its subtabs, and drops the old inline segmented tabs.
- The device library page removes the duplicate identity header and keeps
  subtabs and actions in one compact row. Rockbox themes get an internal
  overview with a compact drop surface, cards with preview/status/metrics/file
  pills, light-mode contrast, and a mobile fallback without horizontal overflow.
- The sync stage refactors its controls (base selector, section tabs,
  destination), adds a wizard stepper with two columns and a per-track side
  toggle, and keeps global side controls visible without artwork optimization
  locking them.

### Activity center
- Progress upserts no longer duplicate an activity id and clamp the reported
  percentage. Discarding a journal or finishing artwork optimization resolves
  the matching activity entry, and completed/failed/discarded device work stops
  counting as active.
- Hydration maps active persisted activities to interrupted warnings, drops
  interrupted artwork-optimization and non-actionable update entries, and prunes
  history to the latest 100 entries.

### Now Playing and Preferences
- When a surface has no artwork to sample, it borrows the player's current color
  instead of a synthetic per-album hue: an album without a cover inherits the
  now-playing tint, and non-album views (including Preferences) adopt the
  player's color whenever a track is loaded.
- The Bibliotecas preferences tab is redesigned to a flat, sectioned layout that
  matches the Playback tab: Carpetas de música, Rutas de MP3 y volúmenes
  (grouped Conectados / Detectados / Sin clasificar with counts and
  Mounted/Offline status badges), and Respaldo y registro.
